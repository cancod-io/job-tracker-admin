# Reasoning — Real Mad Honey Evening Ritual Lander

## 1. Why this page exists

The Meta ads already sell one idea: mad honey as the evening wind-down without alcohol. What fails after the click is message match — visitors land on pages written for a different job (generic product education, intensity curiosity, or a storefront that assumes they already understand the category).

This lander’s job is to **continue the ad**, not restart the conversation:

- Name the evening ritual the buyer already has (wine/beer/pour/pause).
- Explain what mad honey is in plain language for cold traffic.
- Set honest expectations about calm vs drama and batch variance.
- Prove trust (sourcing + every-batch labs + authenticity).
- Teach a safe first approach (spoon → wait → observe).
- Collapse the certainty blocker with a visible **60-day refund**.
- Hand off to **realmadhoney.com** to buy.

Without this page, the ads’ ritual frame dissolves into “expensive weird honey?” — and cold mobile traffic bounces before desire or trust can form.

## 3. Pre-lander vs lander

**Chose lander.**

Cold, low-awareness Meta traffic needs education *and* a path to buy on one phone scroll. A pre-lander that explains then dumps into a mismatched PDP recreates the stated problem and adds a hop (~95% mobile). Statics like RMH528 arrive with almost no education — a teaser page would strand them. The refund and trust stack belong in the same scroll as the claim.

Checkout remains on the live store (real CTA URL) rather than a fake cart — the lander owns persuasion; the store owns purchase ops.

## 5. First live test

**Hero framing A/B:** ritual-first (“Your evening ritual, without the alcohol”) vs product-education-first (“Wild mad honey for the evening wind-down”).

- **Primary:** CTA click-through to realmadhoney.com (mobile).
- **Secondary:** Bounce / &lt;15s sessions.
- **Call a result:** directional lift over 1–2 weeks of meaningful mobile sample, without a spike in fear-driven safety FAQ thrashing.

This is the highest-leverage unknown: ads themselves split between lifestyle permission and product curiosity.

## 6. Flags / changes in the ads

1. **“Skip the booze” / “Don’t pour another glass”** — strong hooks, weak post-click if the lander must avoid intervention tone. Test softer overlays aligned to “keep the ritual.”
2. **“High” / “euphoric”** — create expectation debt the page cannot legally/ brand-wise pay. Prefer calm/mellow/present in creative too.
3. **RMH103 cessation / AA / doctor subplot** — entertaining, but trains the wrong category (quit aid / medical). Keep story; cut cessation as the moral.
4. **Guaranteed ~30-minute calm** — overclaims vs honest variance; soften to “often / for many.”
5. **3AM insomnia static** — pulls toward sleep treatment; either reframe as restless evening or accept it as the weakest fit for this ritual lander.
6. **Scarcity (“sell out fast”)** — fine if harvest-true; avoid fake countdown patterns that erode lab/trust positioning.
