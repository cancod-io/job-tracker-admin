# Real Mad Honey — Ad Analysis & Landing Strategy

Analysis for Meta evening-ritual / alcohol-alternative ads → single destination page.  
Sources: Practical Assignment PDF; Scripts_and_statics.docx; static creatives; realmadhoney.com (authorized).

---

## A. Assignment summary

**Job:** Design one phone-first page that Meta ads about mad honey as the evening wind-down without alcohol can all point to — and document why.

**Commercial problem:** Creative converts attention; post-click experience is written for a different message (generic product education, intensity, or cessation vibes). Cold visitors bounce before they understand *what* it is, *how* to use it tonight, or *why* this jar is trustworthy and returnable.

**Constraints that shape the page:**

- One URL only (no variants scored).
- ~95% mobile; cold, low category awareness.
- Specialty food — not medicine, not cessation product.
- Guardrails ban high/trip/psychedelic, medical/anxiety treatment claims, “stop drinking,” and guaranteed uniform effects.
- Trust stack that must surface: batch lab testing, sourcing authenticity, **60-day refund**, BP medication + pregnancy notes.

**Success definition for this assignment:** Message-matched structure that continues the ad’s evening-ritual promise, educates without lecturing, collapses certainty/risk objections, and hands off to a real buy path on realmadhoney.com.

**Keep the evening ritual. Remove the cost of alcohol.**

Expand for cold traffic:

> Real Mad Honey is wild rhododendron honey for the quiet end of the day — a small spoon, a real shift for many people, and none of the fog. Specialty food. Start small. Lab-tested every batch. Try it with a 60-day refund.

## E. Purchase blocker

**Primary blocker: certainty of effect.**

Alcohol works predictably. Everything else feels like a maybe. Mad honey’s honest truth (batch variance, individual variance, modest calm) can *increase* that hesitation unless the page:

1. Sets **honest expectations** (clear for some, subtle for others — both normal).
2. Explains **why** strength varies (wild product; labs + published reports).
3. Makes serving feel **controllable** (start small → wait → observe).
4. Puts the **60-day refund** in the decision path so “what if I feel nothing?” is financially safe.

Secondary blockers cold traffic brings:

| Blocker | Page answer |
|---------|-------------|
| What even is this? | Plain-language origin (Nepal/Turkey, rhododendron, grayanotoxins) |
| Is it safe / legal food? | Specialty food framing; safety notes findable; not medicine |
| Why so expensive / is it fake? | Sourcing + batch testing + buy from realmadhoney.com |
| Will I overdo it? | Spoon starting point; wait before more |
| Is this a lecture about drinking? | Upgrade tone only — keep the ritual |

## G. Page type

**Choice: Lander (Option B).**

**Why for this angle and buyer:**

1. **Category ignorance is high.** Cold Meta traffic needs what / why / how / trust / risk-reversal before they’ll happily bounce to a generic PDP. A pre-lander that only educates then sends them to a mismatched product page recreates the original problem.
2. **Purchase intent after a strong ad is fragile.** Extra hop taxes mobile patience (~95% of traffic).
3. **One URL behind eight ads** works better as a complete story than as a teaser — especially when statics (RMH528) arrive with almost no education.
4. **Offer + refund belong in the same scroll** as the promise; certainty blocker dies when refund is co-located with the claim.

**Tradeoff accepted:** Lander must stay focused (ritual frame) and not become a full e-com encyclopedia. CTA hands off to **https://realmadhoney.com/** for cart/checkout rather than inventing a fake cart.

**When pre-lander would win:** If ads were already product-aware and PDP conversion was strong — not the stated problem.

## I. Video usage

**Recommendation: 0–1 harvest/sourcing clip**, not a reel of all ad videos.

- Loading five UGC-style ad videos competes with the lander’s own narrative and hurts mobile performance.
- Prefer **one muted, playsinline** sourcing/harvest clip *if* obtainable from Drive or realmadhoney.com CDN — placed in **Why Real Mad Honey** or **What is mad honey?** as trust proof (matches RMH709).
- Otherwise: **no fake video URLs**. Use static frames — product hero (RMH528), bedside ritual (RMH108) — and document Drive slots as intended upgrades.

**Asset note (this build):** Google Drive folder returns reachable HTML but files are not programmatically downloadable here without auth. Site CDN provides product photography, logo, and harvest-related MP4s — prefer those over invented media.

## J. First CRO test

**Test:** Hero framing — **ritual-first** vs **product-education-first**.

| Variant | Hero thesis |
|---------|-------------|
| A (control / build) | Ritual-first: “Your evening ritual, without the alcohol.” |
| B | Education-first: “Wild mad honey for the evening wind-down.” |

**Primary metric:** CTR on primary CTA → realmadhoney.com (or scroll-to-offer engagement if CTA is same-page anchor).  
**Secondary:** Bounce rate / short sessions on mobile (<15s).  
**Guardrail metrics:** FAQ opens on safety questions; refund section view rate.

**Call a result when:** ≥1–2 weeks or meaningful sample on mobile only; look for directional lift in CTA CTR without spike in refund-related support (qualitative) or safety FAQ rage-clicks suggesting fear.

**Why this test first:** Section order and trust stack are locked by strategy; the biggest open question is whether cold traffic needs the *ritual upgrade* permission frame or the *what is this rare honey* frame above the fold — ads themselves split (lifestyle statics vs research video).

# LANDING PAGE MESSAGE-MATCH RECOMMENDATION

## One-line brief for the build

Build a **mobile-first lander** that continues the Meta evening-ritual ads: keep the pour/pause moment, swap alcohol for a spoon of lab-tested mad honey, set honest expectations, and make the 60-day refund impossible to miss — then send them to realmadhoney.com to buy.

## Message match by ad family

| Traffic from | Above-fold match | Lower-page reinforcement |
|--------------|------------------|--------------------------|
| 109 / 112 / 474 | Ritual without alcohol; calm/mellow | Experience + how-to + refund |
| 108 | Nightcap / quiet home visual language | Ritual section mirrors bedside creative |
| 528 A | Product beauty + “experience” → defined as calm evening | Education fills the static’s gap |
| 528 B | Soft recognition of restless nights — not sleep Rx | FAQ: not a sleep medicine |
| 709 | Trust chips early; education section depth | Sourcing + labs section is “home” |
| 103 | Authenticity + precise serving | Why RMH + how-to; no quit-drinking hero |

## Copy poles (use / avoid)

**Use:** evening ritual, wind-down, nightcap (metaphor), calm, mellow, present, settled, spoon, start small, wait, observe, lab-tested every batch, wild-harvested, Nepal & Turkey, rhododendron, grayanotoxins (plain), potency varies, 60-day refund, specialty food.

**Avoid:** high, trip, psychedelic, stop drinking, skip the booze (as headline), addiction/AA, treats anxiety, cures insomnia, guaranteed effects, daily habit, medical cure language.

## CTA

Primary: **Try the Evening Ritual** → `https://realmadhoney.com/`  
Secondary (in-page): Learn how / see the approach (anchor to how-to).

## Visual direction

Warm, premium, natural, calm. Full-bleed hero with real product/lifestyle. Quiet home — not bar/party. Expressive typography. Atmospheric depth (gradient/texture), not flat SaaS purple or generic cream-terracotta cliché cards.
