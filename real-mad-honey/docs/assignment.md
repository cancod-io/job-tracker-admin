# Real Mad Honey — Assignment Brief (Distilled)

Source: Practical Assignment — Landing Page & CRO Lead (PDF, 7 pages).

## Task

Build the single landing page that Meta **alcohol-alternative / evening wind-down** ads should point at — and explain why it was built that way.

**Problem:** Ads work. Post-click pages do not match the message.

## Deliverables

1. **One page** (pre-lander *or* lander) — single URL behind all eight ads; must open without an account; phone-first (~95% mobile).
2. **Reasoning** covering six checklist questions (weighted heaviest).
3. **AI usage** note (2–3 lines: tools, parts, deliberate human work) + which visuals were AI-generated.

## Product facts (usable)

- Wild-harvested mad honey from **Nepal and Turkey**; bees forage **rhododendron**; nectar contains naturally occurring **grayanotoxins**.
- Mild calming at small servings; specialty **food** (not medicine / not supplement health claims); premium; US/EU/UK/CA storefronts.
- Serving = a **spoon**, not a helping; strength varies by batch/season → lab-test every batch, publish reports, reject outside thresholds.
- Most people describe calm / settled head; some feel very little — both normal.
- **60-day refund** collapses certainty-of-effect objection — keep visible.
- Blood pressure medication note + not for pregnant women must be findable.

## The angle

Evening wind-down **without alcohol**. Buyer wants the **ritual** (marker that day ends), not the alcohol itself.

**Core lever:** Upgrade — “Keep your ritual. Remove the cost.” — not intervention (“Stop drinking.”).

## Audience (from brief)

- 55% men / 45% women; age 28–50 (concentrated 32–45); US primary, UK strong; HHI $65k–$150k.
- Cold traffic; problem→solution aware; know adaptogens/CBD/NA drinks; almost none know mad honey.
- Personas: Him 6:45pm (can’t switch off; beer→two); Her 6:30pm (glass of red; puffiness/guilt).

## Competitive wedge

Alcohol has **certainty of effect**. NA drinks = ritual without effect. Adaptogens = slow/inconsistent. CBD = often nothing. Mad honey must promise a real evening moment without alcohol’s cost — with honesty about variance.

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)

**DO:** substitute ritual for evening drink; evenings not daily habit; start small / wait / observe; honest variance; labs + sourcing as trust; 60-day refund visible; BP meds + pregnancy findable; quiet evening imagery (men and women).

**DO NOT:** medical claims; alcohol reduction/addiction/cessation; words **high / trip / psychedelic**; imply daily use or uniform effect; tell them to **stop drinking**.

## Reasoning checklist (six)

1. Why this page exists / job for this angle.
2. Section-by-section why (purpose, audience, objection, placement).
3. Pre-lander vs lander — and why.
4. How one page serves all eight ads (incl. awkward fits).
5. What to test first live + success criteria.
6. What to flag/change in the ads.
